# Public and Private Research Material

The public site is intended for generally applicable scientific theory,
methods, approved examples, and reusable workflow specifications. It is not
the system of record for confidential research or unpublished project data.

Keep the following private unless explicit authorization permits release:

- unpublished structures, reaction barriers, simulation results, and
  experimental comparisons;
- proprietary force-field parameters, internal source code, and licensed
  input data;
- credentials, SSH keys, private host details, internal paths, and restricted
  HPC instructions;
- collaborator or company information covered by confidentiality or
  intellectual-property agreements.

Before publishing a project-specific example, review the content and its
repository history, confirm permissions, and remove sensitive metadata.
Genericized examples should not be assumed safe if their combination of
details could identify a restricted system or result.
