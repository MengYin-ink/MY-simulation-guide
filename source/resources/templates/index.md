# Templates

Reusable templates for research calculations and documentation.

## Technical documentation template

Use or adapt this outline for a method, software, or workflow guide:

```markdown
# Topic

## Overview
What the method or procedure does and when it is useful.

## Background
Relevant theory and assumptions.

## Prerequisites
Required software, versions, input data, and access.

## Procedure
Reproducible step-by-step instructions.

## Validation
Checks that establish whether the result is reliable.

## Common problems
Known failure modes and diagnostic steps.

## References
Official documentation and relevant publications.

## Tested environment
- Software/version:
- System or platform:
- Last verified:
```

Keep theory, method choice, software commands, complete procedures, and
project-specific interpretation in their respective sections. Link related
pages rather than copying the same explanation into multiple documents.
