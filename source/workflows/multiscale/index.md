# Multiscale Workflows

Workflows that connect methods operating at different length and time scales.

For every transferred parameter, record its physical meaning, units,
reference state, derivation, uncertainty, and validation. In reaction-driven
polymer models, do not equate electronic barriers, rate constants, and
per-step reaction probabilities.

See the project-level [DFT-to-MD integration guide](../../polymerization/multiscale-integration/index).
