# Fundamentals

Physical, chemical, and mathematical foundations for computational materials
science. This section answers why the methods work; implementation details are
covered under [Computational Methods](../methods/index).

Topics include quantum mechanics, statistical mechanics, solid-state physics,
chemical kinetics (including [transition state theory](chemical-kinetics/transition-state-theory)),
and numerical methods. Add detailed pages as they are written and verified,
rather than creating empty pages for every future topic.
