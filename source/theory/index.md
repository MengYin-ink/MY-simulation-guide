# Theoretical Background

Physical, chemical, and mathematical foundations for computational materials
science. This section answers why the methods work; implementation details are
covered under [Computational Methods](../methods/index).

The current focus is polymer chemistry and reaction kinetics. Quantum
chemistry, polymer physics, and statistical mechanics provide supporting
foundations. Detailed pages should distinguish established theory from
project-specific assumptions.
