# Quantum Chemistry

Quantum chemistry provides the microscopic description used to calculate
electronic energies, structures, and reaction pathways.

Foundational topics include the Schrödinger equation, the
Born–Oppenheimer approximation, electronic structure, and potential-energy
surfaces. Practical DFT approximations and numerical validation are covered
under [Computational Methods](../../methods/dft/index); software input syntax
belongs in the relevant [software guide](../../software/index).
