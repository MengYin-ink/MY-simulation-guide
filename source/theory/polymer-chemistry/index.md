# Polymer Chemistry

Polymer chemistry describes how molecular building blocks react and form
chains and networks. Public documentation here should explain general
mechanisms and clearly distinguish them from assumptions made for a
particular model or project.

Key concepts include:

- **Initiation:** generation of reactive chain-carrying species.
- **Propagation:** addition of monomer units to a growing chain.
- **Termination:** loss of reactive chain ends, for example by combination or
  disproportionation in radical systems.
- **Copolymerization:** propagation involving more than one monomer type.
- **Crosslinking:** formation of connections between polymer chains or network
  segments; it is not synonymous with ordinary chain propagation.

The [Polymerization Project](../../polymerization/index) applies these concepts
in a staged computational workflow.
