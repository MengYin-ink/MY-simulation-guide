# Reaction Kinetics

Concepts for describing reaction rates and chemical reaction networks,
including activation energies, rate constants, temperature dependence, and
copolymerization kinetics.

An electronic energy barrier from a static calculation is not automatically
an activation free energy or a rate constant. Connecting calculated barriers
to reaction rates requires stated approximations and, where appropriate,
statistical-mechanical corrections.
