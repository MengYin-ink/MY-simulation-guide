# Research Applications

Examples of how computational methods can address scientific questions in
materials and molecular research.

Application pages should focus on the scientific problem, model assumptions,
physical interpretation, and public or otherwise shareable examples. Keep
unpublished results, confidential collaborations, private data, and
restricted computational details out of this public site.

## Topics

- **Gas sensing:** adsorption, surface charge transfer, and sensing mechanisms.
- **Catalysis:** reaction mechanisms, intermediates, and energy barriers.
- **Energy materials:** defects, ion diffusion, and charge transport.
- **Polymer materials:** interactions, polymerization, phase behavior, and transport.

For reusable calculation steps, see [Simulation Workflows](../workflows/index).
