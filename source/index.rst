MY Simulation Guide
===============================

*An Open Knowledge Base for Computational Materials Science and Automated
Simulation Workflows*

MY Simulation Guide documents the theory, methods, and reproducible workflows
used in computational materials science. Its current focus is polymerization
and crosslinking simulation, connecting molecular structures and reaction
barriers to kinetics, polymer-network formation, morphology, and material
properties.

The guide is organized as a knowledge and standards layer. It describes
scientific assumptions, inputs, outputs, and validation criteria; executable
automation belongs in separately maintained software repositories.
