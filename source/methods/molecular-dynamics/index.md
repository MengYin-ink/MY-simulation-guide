# Molecular Dynamics

Molecular dynamics (MD) propagates a model system through time under a chosen
potential-energy model and integration scheme. MD can characterize structural
relaxation and dynamics, but its results depend on the force field, sampling,
system size, and simulation timescale.

Separate model construction, minimization, equilibration, production,
convergence checks, and trajectory analysis. See the [MD workflow](../../workflows/molecular-dynamics/index)
for a reusable procedure and the [LAMMPS guide](../../software/lammps/index)
for software-specific usage.
