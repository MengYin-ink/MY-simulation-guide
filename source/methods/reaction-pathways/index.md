# Reaction Pathways

Methods for locating and analyzing reaction pathways and candidate transition
states, including path interpolation, saddle-point searches, and validation.

For a path-search result, verify endpoint consistency, convergence, and the
chemical interpretation of the path. A maximum-energy image is not, by
itself, sufficient proof of a first-order saddle point.

See [Transition State Theory](../../theory/reaction-kinetics/transition-state-theory)
for the kinetic interpretation and the
[CI-NEB workflow](../../workflows/ci-neb/index) for a reusable procedure.
