# Reactive Polymerization Methods

Reactive polymerization simulations add or change bonds as a model evolves.
Their outcomes depend on the reaction-site definition, candidate-pair
selection, reaction rule, probability or criterion, topology update, and
post-reaction relaxation.

A method description should make these choices explicit and distinguish
chain propagation from crosslink formation. It should also describe limits
such as timestep resolution, force-field consistency, and the mapping between
physical kinetics and accelerated simulation steps.

The project-specific but publicly shareable application is documented in
[MD Polymerization & Crosslinking](../../polymerization/md-polymerization/index).
