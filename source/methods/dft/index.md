# Density Functional Theory (DFT)

Density functional theory (DFT) is an electronic-structure method used to
study molecules and materials. Method documentation should state the
approximations, model, convergence checks, and limits of each result.

Software-specific DFT input and execution instructions belong in the relevant
[software guide](../../software/index). A reproducible end-to-end calculation
belongs in [Simulation Workflows](../../workflows/index).

For reaction calculations, distinguish electronic energies on a potential
energy surface from thermodynamic activation free energies. A path-search
calculation alone does not establish a rate constant.

The [polymerization project](../../polymerization/dft-reaction-kinetics/index)
shows how generic DFT reaction-path methods fit into a broader workflow.
